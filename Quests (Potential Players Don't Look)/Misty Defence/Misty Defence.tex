    \section{Background}

    The plane of negative energy has started to shift into the material plane and a being known as the Mist of the bramble has taken root in the eastern areas of Farlorret threatening to end the reign of man.

    The players will need to defend against waves of enemies threatening a ritual to imbue old relics of Ilmater. 

    \section{Quest board}


    \section{Details}

    A handful of priests are all part of the ritual. For each surviving priest a relic can be imbued with power. 

    About a dozen rangers are stationed at the barricade. They can either be placed as archers or foot soldiers.

    At the end of the round the up to three archers target a designated foe. To hit: 1d20+nr of archers dealing: 1d8+nr of archers

    At the end of the round the foot soldiers move up to 10ft to a designated range this pushes enemies back up to 15ft. 

    Enemies consider the area around a soldier as difficult terrain

    Soldiers AC is 15 + the number of other soldiers orthogonal to it

    A player can designate a range or foe as a bonus action on their turn.


    Move enemies closer

    Slingers extra movement, aoe and pull effect
