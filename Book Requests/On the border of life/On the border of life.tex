    A Blaze raging, Companions fighting, a civilian saved. Fire envolops all, it's sting all at once then suddenly its gone.

    The sensation of falling, a weave of tiny crystals infinetly far away. Eons and instant all at once.

    A woman wakes up in a field of grass, the grass is only a mummer of what grass ought to be. The woman knew this.

    Endless wastes of spanned in every direction but one, without being visiable a city was out there, just like a stork knows north when spring comes.

    The city was unmentionable in the most banal way, lacking apt discription which would make its blandness justice. 

    The inhabitants did not heed the shaman, they just like the buildings whilst searching for meaning they were just empty husks.

    Paths built towards nowhere took the prospect to a crossroad and a gate. Beyond which colour seemed to still thrive. Soft, welcoming and inevitable.

    Suddenly, a figure which had always stood there guarded the gate. It's multiple mouths echoed in unison to enlighten the druid. 

    The oxen front stated surely, beyond the gate is endless hunting grounds

    The lynxen side snapped, one could not return once one stepped through the gate.

    The Lady asked for time.

    The goaten face bleeted, no more than a tenday can she stay here.

    Six wing covered eyes watched the child return into amongsts the buildings. Looming over her, having stalked her for oh so long.

    A massive form blotten out the non existant sun, mottled, pathy fur covered spasming musculator. Wanting, it desired, he needed the morsal standing beneth it.

    Something kept it from feasting. Kept him from indulging its base desires. Only able to salivate viscous grey slobber behind the prey like a growing shadow. 

    Before either entity was able to snatch the wanderer she was plucked from the scales to return another day.
